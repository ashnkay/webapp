# Privacy Policy — Alister Pinterest Connector

*Last updated: October 5, 2026*

## What this app does

Alister Pinterest Connector is a personal-use integration that lets the owner's AI assistant save pins, create pins, and organize boards on the owner's own Pinterest account. It is built for a single user and is not offered to the public.

## Data we access

With your permission, the app accesses:

- Your Pinterest boards (read and write, to organize and save pins)
- Your pins (read and write, to create and manage pins)
- Basic account information (to identify your account)

## How data is used

Data accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to carry out actions you request through your AI assistant, such as saving a pin to a board or listing your boards. We do not sell, share, or disclose your Pinterest data to any third party.

## Data storage

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to communicate with the Pinterest API on your behalf. No Pinterest content is stored outside of your own Pinterest account longer than necessary to complete a requested action.
