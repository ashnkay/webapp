Alister Pinterest Connector

A personal-use integration connecting the owner's AI assistant to their own Pinterest account.

What it does

Save pins — Ask your assistant to save any pin to one of your boards.
Create pins — Create new pins on your account through simple requests.
Organize boards — Browse and manage your boards without opening the app.
Browse boards — List and explore your boards through your assistant.

How it works

The connector uses the official Pinterest API with your permission. When you ask your AI assistant to do something with Pinterest — like saving a pin — it performs that action on your own account, on your behalf. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Who it's for

This is a single-user personal project. It is not offered to the public.
